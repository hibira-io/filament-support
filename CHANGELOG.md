# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- A chat widget for your website: one script tag, or
  `<x-hibira-support::widget />` in a Blade layout. Messages arrive live over
  Hibira, with polling as a fallback.
- A Support inbox page in the Filament panel: open, mine and closed
  conversations, unread badge, take over, hand back to the AI, close, and a
  notification anywhere in the panel when a visitor needs the team.
- AI answers with your own API key: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini,
  OpenRouter, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible service such as Ollama. Off,
  suggested replies, or automatic answers that hand over to the team.
- Limits on AI answers per conversation, per visitor and per day.
- English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, ไทย and Tiếng Việt.
